Three looks for the OTSO film

Each look gets two frames rendered in Remotion, the same engine as the final film: the hero reveal, plus one beat on a real OTSO screen. The story stays the same (problem, then the fix). Only the visual world changes, and none of them reuse ShingTik's cream and gold.

A · Midnight Vault

Recommended
World
A dark archive in deep space. Documents are frosted-glass slabs, and the AI is a cobalt light beam that lights up whatever it reads. Compliance becomes a vault door closing. Fintech launch film.
Camera
Slow fly-throughs between the slabs, rim-lit product windows, floor reflections.
Music
Dark cinematic electronic pulse.
Colour
#070B18#0D1836#3D7BFF#9DBBFF

Why I recommend it: OTSO is a regulated brokerage, and this look reads as secure and premium. It sits naturally next to OTSO's own navy product, and it's the furthest from the light ShingTik film.

B · Ink & Paper

World
White paper and graphite. Documents are sketched live by a pen line, and cobalt ink floods colour into whatever the AI understands. Screens stay greyscale except for the part the AI is doing.
Camera
Mostly flat and editorial, with hand-drawn arrows, circles and underlines.
Music
Minimal piano and plucks.
Colour
#FDFDFB#24262B#9A9DA4#1E46F0

C · Infinite Canvas

World
One giant design-tool canvas. Every OTSO screen is an artboard, and a blinking violet-to-blue caret leads the camera from feature to feature in one continuous glide. Selection boxes and minimap details.
Camera
One long continuous pan and zoom, with no hard cuts.
Music
Upbeat indie electronic.
Colour
#E8ECF2#111827#7C5CFF#2F7BFF

All three keep the end card with the Codech logo, contacts and WhatsApp QR. It's restyled to whichever look you pick.
