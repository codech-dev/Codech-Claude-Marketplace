## What to post (in this order)

| # | When | Where | File | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 1, 8–9 pm MYT | Instagram Reel + Facebook Reel (cross-post from Meta Business Suite) | `<slug>-reel-9x16-short.mp4` (0:30–0:45) | The hook post. Short Reels finish more often, and completion drives reach. |
| 2 | Day 1, right after | Instagram Story + Facebook Story | Share post 1 to Stories, add a **Link sticker** to the case study | Stories are the only place on Instagram where a link is tappable. |
| 3 | Day 3–4 | Instagram Reel + Facebook | `<slug>-reel-9x16-full.mp4` | The full story for people who want the detail (challenge → solution → delivery → results). |
| 4 | Any time | LinkedIn / YouTube / website | `<slug>-16x9-short.mp4` and the 16:9 master | Landscape screens: LinkedIn feed, YouTube, presentations, the website Results card. |

- **Covers:** upload `cover-9x16.jpg` as the Reel cover. Its title sits inside the centre 3:4 area, so it still reads in the Instagram profile grid crop.
- **Pin** post 1 to the top of the Instagram profile and the Facebook page for the first two weeks.
- **Collab post:** if <Client> agrees, send their Instagram account a Collab invite on post 1. It then appears on both profiles and reaches both audiences. Tag their Facebook page in the caption either way.
- **Muted viewing:** most people watch with sound off. Every scene carries its own headline, so no subtitles are needed.

## Before posting

- **Is what the film shows actually live?** If any feature shown as working is still in build, wait for the launch or check the wording with <Client>. The posts are public and tag them.
- **<Client>'s handles:** add their official Instagram and Facebook handles.
- **Consent:** `client.consent_confirmed` must be true before naming the client in public posts.
- **Music:** <track name> by <artist>, <Mixkit / Pixabay> (track <id>). Their licences allow commercial use with no credit needed. If Facebook or Instagram flags the audio, dispute it with the licence page. Don't swap in Instagram's in-app music: business accounts only get the Meta Sound Collection, and replacing the audio breaks the sound-effect timing.
- **Sample data:** any dashboard figures shown as sample data in the film must not be quoted as real results in captions.
